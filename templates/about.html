About WhatIsLit?

Our Story

WhatIsLit? was born during the pandemic when we realized how much we missed discovering new, exciting places to hang out. Stuck at home, we dreamed of the day when we could explore the hottest spots in our city again. When restrictions lifted, we found ourselves overwhelmed by choice - there were so many places to go, but which ones were actually worth our time?

That's when the idea hit us: what if there was a way to instantly find the most popular, "lit" venues in any area? We wanted to cut through the noise and show people exactly where the action is happening right now.

What We Do

WhatIsLit? uses real-time popularity data to show you the hottest spots in any city or neighborhood. We analyze foot traffic, check-ins, and social media buzz to give you an accurate picture of where people are actually going and having a great time.

Whether you're looking for a trendy new restaurant, a buzzing bar, or the hottest club in town, we help you find places that are actually worth your time and money.

How It Works

1. Search: Enter any city, neighborhood, or zipcode you want to explore

2. Choose: Select the type of venue you're interested in (restaurant, bar, club, etc.)

3. Discover: Get a list of the most popular spots ranked by real-time activity

4. Explore: Use our interactive map to see exactly where these hot spots are located

Our Mission

We believe that great experiences happen at the intersection of good company and great venues. Our mission is to help you discover those perfect spots where memories are made, whether you're planning a night out with friends, a date, or just want to explore what your city has to offer.
