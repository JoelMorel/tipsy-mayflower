Frequently Asked Questions

How often is the data updated?

Our popularity data is updated in real-time, so you're always seeing the most current information about where people are going.

Do you cover all cities?

We're constantly expanding our coverage! We currently support major cities and metropolitan areas, with new locations added regularly.

Is WhatIsLit? free to use?

Yes! WhatIsLit? is completely free to use. We believe everyone deserves to find the best spots in their city.

How do you determine if a place is "lit"?

We analyze multiple data sources including foot traffic, check-ins, social media mentions, and real-time activity to determine popularity.
